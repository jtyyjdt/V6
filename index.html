11 January 2027

Dear Bubu,

One year sounds like a simple number until I look at everything that happened inside it. Then it stops feeling like a number and starts feeling like a whole little world.

A world that started with us finding our way back to each other, and then somehow becoming something neither of us could have completely imagined.

I still think about that night. 11 January 2026. Around 1 AM, when a simple question turned into the beginning of us.

And then, at 1:33 AM, you gave me those words that I don't think I'll ever forget. That was the moment everything changed.

Our first year wasn't perfect. We had beautiful days, but we had difficult ones too. We fought. We cried. We misunderstood each other. Sometimes circumstances made things harder than either of us wanted.

But somehow, again and again, we found our way back to each other. And I think that is one of the most beautiful things about our story.

I remember the difficult day on 26 January, and how you were there for me. I remember the late-night conversations, the long calls, the moments when we laughed for no reason, and the times when simply talking to you made an ordinary day feel different.

I remember the days when we missed each other, the days when communication became difficult, and the days when we had to learn how to understand each other again.

And I remember 8 August too — meeting again, hugging, laughing, taking pictures, and having a day that felt beautifully simple.

Somewhere along the way, we didn't just collect memories. We grew. We learned. We became closer. We shared deeply personal moments, and we learned that being close also means respecting each other's feelings and boundaries.

Bubu, I don't want to promise you a perfect future, because I don't know what every tomorrow will look like.

But I can promise to keep trying. To listen when you need to be heard. To communicate instead of letting silence become a wall. To respect your feelings, your boundaries, and your dreams. To learn from my mistakes instead of repeating them.

I promise to keep appreciating the little things — the conversations, the laughter, the random moments, the quiet moments, and all the tiny pieces of life that somehow became our memories.

I don't know exactly where life will take us. But I know that this first year will always be one of the most meaningful chapters of my life.

And if I could go back to the beginning, knowing everything that was waiting for us — every beautiful moment, every difficult one, every laugh, every tear, every lesson — I would still choose this story.

And I would still choose you.

One year down. So much more left to write.

Happy First Anniversary, Bubu.

Always keep a little space in your heart for all the memories we haven't made yet.

K
